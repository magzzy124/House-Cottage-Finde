About us

HouseFinder was built as a university project to demonstrate a full-stack real estate listing platform. It allows users to browse, search, and compare houses and cottages across Serbia and the surrounding region.

The platform provides a seamless experience for both buyers and renters. Whether you are looking for a city apartment in Belgrade, a suburban family house, or a seaside cottage in Montenegro, HouseFinder helps you discover the right property.

Registered users can save their favorite listings, compare multiple properties side-by-side, and explore market statistics to make informed decisions.
